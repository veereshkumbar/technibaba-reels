# The 5 script formats (every reel uses exactly one)

Source: a "5 formats" breakdown attributed to Tanmay Bhat (reel by yash_socialparadigm, 6 Nov 2026, 2K likes, 3.4K shares). Each row is one format; read it left to right as the order of the script.

| # | Format | Beat 1 (0–3 s) | Beat 2 (3–8 s) | Beat 3 (the "stay") | Beat 4 (payoff) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Results first** | Show the result | How you did it | Reason to stay | What they learn |
| 2 | **Not A, not B, actually C** | "It's not A" (the obvious answer) | "Not B either" (the second guess) | "It's actually C" | Stay: why C, what to do |
| 3 | **Common belief** | State what everyone believes | Contradict it | Stay: "here's the proof" | Why it's wrong / what's true |
| 4 | **Better way** | "There's a better way to do X" | "...but the best way is Y" | Re-hook ("and the third one surprised me") | Keep them watching to the end |
| 5 | **Won't work, unless** | "X won't work" | "...unless you do this" | Do this (the steps) | Stay: the one mistake to avoid |

## How we use them
- **Pick the format before writing.** In SCHEDULED_RUN.md step 3, write the format number next to the chosen hook. Rotate: never use the same format in two slots on the same day.
- **Fit to lane:** tutorials → 5 (won't work, unless) or 1 (results first); scam/safety → 2 (not A, not B, actually C) or 3 (common belief); trending tech/sports/Bollywood → 3 or 2; comparisons and tools → 4 (better way, best way).
- **Beat 1 is the hook and must land by 1.5 s**, on screen as text too (most people watch muted).
- **The "stay" beat is an explicit open loop**, said out loud: "aur teesra wala sabse important hai", "last step mat chhodna", "proof dekho". It sits right before the point where people leave (8–12 s) and replaces dead air.
- **The payoff (C, the best way, the fix) arrives by 10 s** (SCHEDULED_RUN.md "Engagement rules"); the mid-video share ask goes right after it.
- **Don't fake it:** "not A, not B" must be real wrong guesses people actually make, and "common belief" must actually be common. Never invent a contradiction for drama.

## Examples (Hinglish)
1. "Ye reel 0 rupaye mein bani. Kaise? 4 tools. Last wala free hai aur kisi ko pata nahi."
2. "UPI se paise hacker ne nahi nikale. Nakli app ne bhi nahi. Aapne khud PIN daala, paise *lene* ke liye."
3. "Sab bolte hain phone ko raat bhar charge mat karo. Aaj ke phones mein ye galat hai, proof dekho."
4. "Storage full? Photos delete karna theek hai. Isse better: cache saaf karo. Best: ye ek setting."
5. "Strong password se Instagram safe nahi hoga. Jab tak two-factor on nahi karoge. 3 step, 30 second."

# The 5 script formats (every reel uses exactly one)

Source: "Tanmay Bhat uses only these 5 formats to make any video viral" (reel by yash_socialparadigm, 6 Nov 2026, 2K likes, 3.4K shares). Re-studied 19 Nov from the full narration (transcribed), not just the table.

## What the reel actually says (narration, cleaned up)
1. "Show the **result** first, then show **how you did it**. They **stay** to **learn** how you did it."
2. "Say it's **not A**, it's **neither B**, it's **actually C**. They **stay** because they want to know **why** it's C."
3. "Talk about a **common belief** of the audience, then **contradict** it. They **stay** because they want to know **why**."
4. "Say this is a **better way**, but here is the **best way**. You are **re-hooking** them, and they **watch till the end**."
5. "Say this **won't work, unless** you do this. They **stay** because they want to know **how it will work**."

## The real rule behind all 5: plant one question, answer it last
The table's columns are: **beat 1 → beat 2 → "Stay" → the reason they stay.** "Stay" is not a line we say; it is the *effect*: beats 1 and 2 put a question in the viewer's head (how? why C? why is the belief wrong? what's the best way? how does it work?). The rest of the reel answers that question, and the viewer stays to get the answer.

| # | Format | Beat 1 (hook, 0–2 s) | Beat 2 (by ~6 s) | Question planted in the viewer's head | Body + ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Results first | Show the finished result | "Here's how I did it" | *How did they do that?* | The steps; the last step completes the result |
| 2 | Not A, not B, actually C | "It's not A" (the obvious guess) | "Not B either. It's C." | *Why is it C?* | The reasons C is true, the proof last |
| 3 | Common belief | State what the audience believes | Contradict it | *Why is that wrong?* | The proof / mechanism, the correct version last |
| 4 | Better way → best way | "This way is good/better" | "But here's the best way" (re-hook) | *What's the best way?* | Best way revealed and shown fully at the end |
| 5 | Won't work, unless | "X won't work" | "...unless you do this" | *How does "this" make it work?* | The method; the moment it works is the ending |

How this fits our engagement rules (SCHEDULED_RUN.md): the **claim** (the result, C, the contradiction, "best way exists", "unless") is said and shown by 6–10 s. That is the payoff the hook promised, and it is what stops the swipe. The **why/how** is the body and is completed only at the end. The mid-video share ask goes right after the claim, while the question is still open.

## Rules for every scheduled reel
- **Pick the format before writing** (SCHEDULED_RUN.md step 3) and write two lines in the notes: the format number, and the **question the viewer will have at 6 s** in their own words ("why is the PIN the problem?"). If you can't write that question, the script has no reason to stay. Rewrite it.
- **Rotate:** never the same format in two slots on the same day (check posted.md).
- **Fit to lane:** tutorials → 1 or 5; scams/safety → 2 or 3; trending tech/sports/Bollywood → 3 or 2; tools/comparisons → 4.
- **Beat 1 lands by 1.5 s**, spoken and on screen (most people watch muted).
- **Never answer the planted question early.** If the why/how is fully given before 60% of the reel, there's no reason to stay; move it later.
- **Format 4 needs two real options:** the "better way" must be genuinely decent, so the re-hook to "best" feels earned.
- **Answer it fully at the end:** the last 20% closes the loop clearly, then one ask. An unanswered question at the end feels like a cheat and kills shares.
- **Don't fake it:** "not A, not B" must be guesses people really make; the "common belief" must really be common; the result in format 1 must be real.

## Worked examples (Hinglish)
1. "Ye reel 0 rupaye mein bani." (result) → "4 free tools, dikhata hoon kaise." → steps, last tool reveals the trick.
2. "UPI se paise hacker ne nahi nikale. Nakli app ne bhi nahi. Aapne khud PIN daala." → *why does my PIN matter?* → "Paise lene mein PIN kabhi nahi lagta..." → the collect-request trick → decline + 1930.
3. "Sab bolte hain phone raat bhar charge mat karo." → "Aaj ke phones mein ye galat hai." → *why?* → battery chip / optimized charging → what actually harms the battery.
4. "Storage full? Photos delete karna theek hai." → "Par best tareeka koi nahi batata." → *what is it?* → cache, then the one setting, shown fully at the end.
5. "Strong password se Instagram safe nahi hoga." → "...jab tak two-factor on nahi karoge." → *how?* → 3 steps, ending on the "account protected" screen.

WhatsApp pe 3 step:
1. WhatsApp > Settings > Privacy > Groups > "My contacts" chuno (Everyone hatao)
2. Ab anjaan number aapko add nahi kar sakta, sirf private chat mein invite bhej sakta hai (3 din mein expire). Invite mat kholna
3. Settings > Privacy > Calls > "Silence unknown callers" ON

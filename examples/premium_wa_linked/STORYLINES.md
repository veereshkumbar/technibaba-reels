# Storylines (3) and hooks (3) — 25 Nov 2026, 11:00 tutorial

One thing the viewer must remember: **WhatsApp par koi doosra device juda ho sakta hai, aur Linked devices se do tap
mein nikal jaata hai.**

## Storylines
1. **Result first (CHOSEN).** Baba already has the fix on screen: an unknown laptop sitting in his Linked devices
   list, then gone, list empty. Chotu panics about his own phone. Then the two taps, step by step, and the empty
   list closes the loop at the end. Why: format 1 needs a real finished result in beat 1, and "list khaali" is a
   result you can show in one frame, muted.
2. **The cyber-cafe moment.** Chotu remembers printing a form at a cyber cafe last month; his chats are still open on
   that computer. Rejected: it puts the payoff behind a story, and half the audience has never used a cyber cafe.
3. **Before / after the list.** Four devices linked vs an empty list, as a comparison page. Rejected: a comparison is
   not a result; nothing happens on screen, so the muted read is weak.

## Hooks
A. "Ye dekho. Ek anjaan laptop mere WhatsApp se bahar. Do tap mein." — CHOSEN. The result is on screen at frame 0,
   and "anjaan laptop" is the thing that makes a person stop.
B. "Aapke WhatsApp mein abhi bhi kisi ka laptop juda ho sakta hai." — rejected: this is the consumer-desk hook every
   outlet used this week, and it accuses without showing anything.
C. "Cyber cafe mein tab band kiya? WhatsApp wahan 30 din tak chalu hai." — the strongest *fact*, so it is not the
   hook; it is the claim at ~7 s, where the format wants it.

Share target, named: the friend or cousin who opens WhatsApp on someone else's laptop.

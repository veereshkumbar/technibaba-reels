Phone mein reel ekdum saaf, Instagram pe daalte hi dhundhli? Ye 3 fix karo.

1. Setting: Profile > Menu > Settings and privacy > Media quality > Upload at highest quality ON (Use less mobile data OFF)
2. Size: 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, MP4. 4K ka fayda nahi, Instagram usse chhota kar deta hai
3. Upload: achhe WiFi pe, original file. WhatsApp se aayi copy nahi, woh video daba deta hai

Us dost ko bhejo jiski har reel dhundhli aati hai, aur comment karo GUIDE, poora rasta DM mein bhejenge.

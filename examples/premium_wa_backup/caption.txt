Google storage full, toh WhatsApp backup band? Ab zaroori nahi.

Reports ke hisaab se WhatsApp apna cloud backup la raha hai, Google Drive ke bina:
- 2 GB free, WhatsApp ke apne server pe
- Rasta: WhatsApp > Settings > Chats > Chat backup > Cloud backup > WhatsApp chuno
- Abhi sirf kuch Android beta users ko mila hai, dheere dheere sabko aayega
- Backup hamesha lock (end-to-end encrypted): passkey ya 64 ank ki key. Bhool gaye toh WhatsApp bhi backup nahi khol sakta

Papa ko bhejo, jinka phone roz "storage full" bolta hai. Aap kaunsa rakhoge: GOOGLE ya WHATSAPP? Comment karo.

Feature is in beta (Android 2.26.46.4) and may not be on your phone yet; menu labels can change.

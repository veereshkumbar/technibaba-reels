Call forwarding scam se bachne ke 4 step:
1. MAT DIAL: anjaan ka diya code kabhi dial mat karo, khaaskar jo 21, 61 ya 67 se shuru ho (jaise *21*number#)
2. CHECK: Android: Phone app > Settings > Call forwarding (kuch phones mein Calls ya Calling accounts ke andar). iPhone: Settings > Phone > Call Forwarding
3. BAND: ##002# dial karo, saari forwarding band. Jio pe *402 bhi
4. Paise kate? Turant 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in

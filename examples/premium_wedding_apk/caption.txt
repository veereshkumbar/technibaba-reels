Shaadi ka card WhatsApp pe aaya, aur file ke end mein .apk likha hai? Woh card nahi, app hai.

Asli card PDF, photo ya video hota hai. Android pe 4 step:
1. Settings mein search karo "Install unknown apps" > WhatsApp > Allow from this source OFF. Chrome aur Files bhi OFF
2. Play Store > profile icon > Play Protect > Settings > Scan apps with Play Protect ON
3. Phone bole "Install blocked"? Cancel dabao. Bhejne wala bole "Settings dabao", toh wahin ruko aur pehle usse call karke poocho
4. Galti se khul gaya? Net band, app uninstall, bank ko call, aur 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in

Jeevan Pramaan face scan, 4 step:
1. Google Play se 2 app: Jeevan Pramaan aur AadhaarFaceRD (iska icon nahi dikhta, ye normal hai)
2. Operator yaani aap: ek baar apna Aadhaar, mobile, OTP aur face scan
3. Pensioner ka Aadhaar, mobile, OTP, phir PPO number, bank aur pension office details, preview check karke Submit
4. Pensioner ka face scan: seedha dekho, aankhein khuli, achhi roshni. Pramaan ID aur SMS aayega. Baad mein jeevanpramaan.gov.in pe certificate download karke accept/reject check karo

Aadhaar ka nakli angootha, bina OTP ke paise nikaal sakta hai. Jab tak Biometric Lock on nahi.

2 minute ka kaam:
1. myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in khud type karo > Login: Aadhaar number, captcha, OTP
2. Lock/Unlock Biometrics > box pe tick > Lock
3. Bank ya ration pe angootha lagana hai? Wahin se 10 minute ka Unlock, phir apne aap lock
4. Paise kate? Turant 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in

Awaaz aayi, umpire ne OUT diya... phir bhi NOT OUT? Snicko ke 3 check.

Aaj (19 Nov) Wellington Test mein Bumrah ke doosre over mein Devon Conway ko caught behind diya gaya. Review mein dikha ki awaaz, ball ke bat tak pahunchne se pehle aayi thi. Faisla palta: NOT OUT.
- SPIKE: stump mic awaaz pakadta hai. Ball bat ko chhue toh graph pe tez spike
- FRAME: spike usi frame mein ho jab ball bat ke paas hai. Pehle ya baad mein = bat ki awaaz nahi
- SHAPE: ball-bat = patla, tez spike. Pad pe lage ya bat zameen pe lage = mota, phaila

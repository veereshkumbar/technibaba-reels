Hindi reel, par captions Hindi aur English dono mein? Edits app khud bana deta hai.

Edits app mein Bilingual captions, 4 step:
1. Edits app lo (Meta ka, free, Android aur iPhone)
2. Video daalo, neeche toolbar mein Captions dabao
3. Bilingual captions dabao, doosri bhasha chuno, jaise English
4. Generate captions dabao, phir har line padh ke check karo
15 bhasha milti hain, Hindi, English, Bengali, Gujarati aur Kannada bhi. Doosri bhasha machine translation hai, galti ho sakti hai.

Save karo, aur us dost ko bhejo jo Hindi mein reels banata hai. Comment karo GUIDE, step-by-step guide DM mein bhejenge.

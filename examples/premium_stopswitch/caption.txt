Scam hua toh paise gaye, ab kuch nahi ho sakta? RBI ka naya draft kuch aur kehta hai.
- Stop switch: ek tap, khaate se har naya outgoing payment band. Paise aana chalu, EMI aur auto-pay chalte rahenge.
- Trusted person: aapki set ki hui limit se bada transfer tabhi jaayega jab woh 1 ghante mein haan bole (UPI, shop aur auto-pay payments is mein shamil nahi).
- Abhi ye sirf draft hai (23 Nov 2026), feedback 14 Dec tak. Report ke hisaab se banks ko 1 July 2027 tak dena hoga, chahein toh pehle.
Source: RBI draft directions, reported by Business Standard, MediaNama, Republic.

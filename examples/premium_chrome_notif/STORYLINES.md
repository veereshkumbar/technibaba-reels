# Storylines (3) and hooks (3) — 26 Nov 2026, 11:00 tutorial

Remember: **"Virus detected" alerts on the phone are usually a website you allowed to send notifications. Chrome > Site settings > Notifications > Block.**
Format 2. Question at 6 s: "Allow dabane se virus wala alert kaise aata hai, aur band kaise hoga?"

## Storylines
1. **Not virus, not hacker, actually Allow (CHOSEN).** Chotu panics about Papa's phone; Baba rules out the two guesses everybody makes and names the real cause, then 3 steps. Why: the two wrong guesses are real (people install "cleaner" apps or factory-reset), and "send to Papa" is obvious.
2. **Result first:** a clean notification shade, then how. Rejected: format 1 was yesterday's tutorial.
3. **Belief: "antivirus app lagao".** Rejected: needs product claims about antivirus apps we can't verify in one run.

## Hooks
A. "Baba! Papa ke phone pe har ghante Virus Detected aa raha hai!" — CHOSEN: the exact alert people have seen, and names the share target.
B. "Ye virus nahi hai. Ye aapka Allow hai." — weaker without the face/panic.
C. "Aapke phone mein 10 websites ghus ke baithi hain." — sounds like an exaggeration.
